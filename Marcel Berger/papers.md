The point of this file is to identify durable source trails and possible picture hooks. The hook is not a substitute for the theorem.

## 1955 — holonomy

**Sur les groupes d'holonomie homogènes de variétés à connexion affine et des variétés riemanniennes**  
Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France 83 (1955), 279–330.  
PDF: https://www.numdam.org/article/BSMF_1955__83__279_0.pdf

Why it matters: Berger's holonomy work is central to the classification of possible irreducible Riemannian holonomy groups.

Picture-sized entry point before any classification theorem:

- put a tangent vector at a point;
- parallel-transport it around several closed loops;
- compare the rotations obtained when it comes back;
- only then introduce “holonomy” as the collection generated by such returns.

This is an explanation-level entry point, not yet a page-audited extraction from the 1955 proof.

## 1960 — quarter pinching

**Les variétés riemanniennes (1/4)-pincées**  
Annali della Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa, 14 (1960), 161–170.  
Source: https://numdam.org/item/ASNSP_1960_3_14_2_161_0/

Possible picture hook:

- show several directions through one point on a surface-like model and attach sectional-curvature gauges;
- squeeze the allowed curvature range toward the ratio 1:4;
- explain that the actual theorem is higher-dimensional and the surface picture is only a model for the meaning of a curvature bound.

Do **not** present a two-dimensional drawing as a proof of the sphere theorem.

Status: source located; exact theorem/passage still needs page audit before scripting.

## 1972 — systolic geometry

**Du côté de chez Pu**  
Annales scientifiques de l'École Normale Supérieure 5 (1972), 1–44.  
Source: https://numdam.org/articles/10.24033/asens.1219/

Promising conceptual lane: compare global area/volume with the length of the shortest topologically essential closed curve. This can become a picture of loops tightening on a surface while the surface's total area stays visible.

Before using a numerical inequality, verify the exact normalization and hypotheses in Berger's text.

Status: source located; inequality-level details need page audit.

## 1983 — just below one-quarter pinching

**Sur les variétés riemanniennes pincées juste au-dessous de 1/4**  
Annales de l'Institut Fourier 33 no. 2 (1983), 135–150.  
Source: https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.5802/aif.920/  
Erratum: https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.5802/aif.927/

This is also a useful Berger ↔ Gromov bridge: Berger explicitly uses a Gromov compactness/convergence theorem in the argument.

Possible future Short:

2. show the idea of extracting a convergent geometric limit;
3. stop before pretending that the visual sequence proves Berger's rigidity conclusion.
