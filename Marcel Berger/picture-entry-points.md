## Notes for later extraction

- Do not make “Berger proved X” the hook if the picture only explains a definition used near X.
- Prefer an exact Berger diagram/construction when one exists, but redraw rather than simply displaying a scanned page unless the source/license makes reuse appropriate.
- A Short can end at the question. For example, “How evenly can five points be placed on a sphere?” is already a coherent mathematical object without racing through a general solution.
- When a chapter yields several independent constructions, split them. There is no chapter-to-video mapping.
