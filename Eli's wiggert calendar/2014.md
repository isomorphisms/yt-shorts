# Complex Beauties 2014 — source-audit lesson seeds

| February | **Liouville and complex analysis.** **Exact picture audit:** determine whether the page uses Liouville's theorem, an entire-function construction, or another namesake result. | **Joseph Liouville.** His work runs from bounded entire functions to transcendental numbers, differential equations, and mechanics; the calendar page decides which thread belongs here. | [Liouville's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liouville%27s_theorem_(complex_analysis)) · [Joseph Liouville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Liouville). |
| March | **Newton iteration**, including Simpson's early use of Newton's method for systems. | **Thomas Simpson.** Simpson's 1740 *Essays in Mathematicks* applied Newton-style iteration beyond a single polynomial equation, long before modern numerical-analysis language. | [Newton's method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton%27s_method) · historical work on Simpson's extension of Newton iteration. |
| April | **Gauss topic — exact picture audit.** Do not infer Gaussian quadrature, hypergeometric functions, or another Gauss namesake from the portrait alone. | **Carl Friedrich Gauss.** Use the original page to pick the correct branch of his unusually broad work. | [Gauss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Gauss). |
| May | **Hadamard topic — exact picture audit.** Likely lesson graph includes entire functions/factorization, but the displayed construction must be verified first. | **Jacques Hadamard.** Hadamard connected zero sets, growth of entire functions, and prime-number asymptotics; the exact calendar function should determine the emphasis. | [Hadamard factorization theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadamard_factorization_theorem) · [Hadamard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Hadamard). |

| July | **Mittag-Leffler function `E_α(z)`** and its interpolation between exponential-like and more slowly growing behavior. | **Gösta Mittag-Leffler.** The namesake special function came from his work on summability/analytic continuation and later became central in fractional differential equations. | [Mittag-Leffler function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mittag-Leffler_function) · Gorenflo et al., *Mittag-Leffler Functions, Related Topics and Applications*. |

| September | **Stern–Brocot sequence/tree.** | **Moritz Abraham Stern.** Stern's number sequence and Brocot's independently developed tree encode every positive rational exactly once; the arithmetic recursion is highly animatable. | [Stern–Brocot tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stern%E2%80%93Brocot_tree) · [Stern's diatomic series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stern%27s_diatomic_series). |
| October | **Frostman topic — exact picture audit.** Candidate graph: Blaschke products, boundary behavior, Frostman shifts, potential theory. | **Otto Frostman.** His work links analytic functions to capacity and potential theory. | [Frostman's lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frostman%27s_lemma) · standard texts on inner functions/Blaschke products. |
| November | **Painlevé transcendents.** Guest contribution credited in the 2014 calendar introduction to Bengt Fornberg. | **Paul Painlevé.** Classifying second-order ODEs by movable singularities produced six new transcendental families; modern asymptotic and numerical work treats them as nonlinear special functions. | [Painlevé transcendents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Painlev%C3%A9_transcendents) · Ince, *Ordinary Differential Equations*; modern numerical work by Fornberg and collaborators. |
| December | **Runge approximation / polynomial approximation.** **Exact picture audit:** recover the displayed function and domain from the page. | **Carl David Tolmé Runge.** His approximation theorem says holomorphic functions can be approximated by rational functions with poles placed outside the domain; his name also survives in Runge's interpolation phenomenon. | [Runge's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runge%27s_theorem) · [Runge's phenomenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runge%27s_phenomenon). |
