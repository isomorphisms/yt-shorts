# Complex Beauties 2016 — lesson seeds

Primary source: [official *Complex Beauties* archive](https://blogs.hrz.tu-freiberg.de/mathekalender/english/) and the author-uploaded 2016 calendar. Calendar assets remain external.

| Month | Function / pictured object | Person / historical story | Reading and citation trail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| January | **Chebyshev polynomials `T_n`** and interpolation nodes. | **Pafnuty Chebyshev.** The minimax/equioscillation geometry explains why Chebyshev nodes tame the edge blow-up of equally spaced interpolation. | [Chebyshev polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chebyshev_polynomials) · [Runge's phenomenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runge%27s_phenomenon). |
| February | **Iteration of holomorphic maps; Julia and Fatou sets.** | **Pierre Fatou.** Fatou and Julia developed normal-family methods for iteration decades before computer pictures made the sets famous. | [Fatou set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatou_set) · Hubbard, *The Mandelbrot Set, Theme and Variations* (LMS 274, 2000). |
| March | **Belyi maps / dessins d'enfants**; a rational map encodes a planar graph through the preimage of `[0,1]`. Guest contribution by Donald Marshall. | **Gennadii Belyi.** Belyi's theorem ties algebraic curves over number fields to branched covers with only three critical values. | [Belyi's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belyi%27s_theorem) · [Dessins d'enfants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dessin_d%27enfant). |
| April | **p-valent functions**, pictured with a rotated `z^5(1-z)^(-10)`. | **Mary Lucy Cartwright.** Her value-distribution work and the wartime nonlinear-radio problem with Littlewood helped launch modern chaos questions. | [Mary Cartwright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Cartwright) · W. K. Hayman, *Bull. LMS* 34 (2002); McMurran & Tattersall, *Notices AMS* 46(2) (1999). |
| May | **Tricomi confluent hypergeometric function**, pictured `U(2,11,z)`. | **Francesco Giacomo Tricomi.** Hypergeometric equations organize a large family of named special functions; Tricomi's `U` is selected by its behavior at infinity. | [Confluent hypergeometric function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confluent_hypergeometric_function) · NIST DLMF Chapter 13. |
| June | **Polygamma function**, calendar picture `ψ_6`. Guest contribution by Jörn Steuding. | **Adolf Hurwitz.** Hurwitz's zeta-function work leads naturally to polygamma identities through derivatives of `log Γ`. | [Polygamma function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polygamma_function) · [Hurwitz zeta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurwitz_zeta_function). |
| July | **Meijer G-function**, one `G^{1,1}_{2,2}` example. | **Cornelis Simon Meijer.** One contour-integral notation contains huge families of classical special functions as special cases. | [Meijer G-function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meijer_G-function) · NIST DLMF §16.17. |
| August | **Moment function of a four-step planar random walk**, analytically continued with a Meijer G-function. | **Karl Pearson.** Pearson posed the random-walk problem in *Nature* in 1905; a century later its densities and moments led to unexpectedly rich special functions. | [Random walk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_walk) · Borwein et al., “Densities of short uniform random walks,” *Canad. J. Math.* 64(5) (2012). |
| September | **Whittaker fixed-derivative iteration** `z_{k+1}=z_k-λf(z_k)` and acceleration. | **E. T. Whittaker.** A root-finder that freezes the derivative trades local cost against convergence behavior—easy to compare visually with Newton's method. | [E. T. Whittaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._T._Whittaker) · M. A. Hernández, 1990 work on Whittaker's method. |
| October | **Almost periodic functions.** Guest contribution by Jörn Steuding. | **Harald Bohr.** Bohr generalized trigonometric polynomials to functions with approximate rather than exact periods, motivated partly by analytic number theory. | [Almost periodic function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almost_periodic_function) · Bohr's 1924–25 papers. |
| November | **Blasius function**, solving `2f''' + f f'' = 0`. | **Paul Richard Heinrich Blasius.** Boundary-layer flow turns the Navier–Stokes equations into a nonlinear ODE; its complex singularities reveal what the real engineering solution hides. | [Blasius boundary layer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blasius_boundary_layer) · J. P. Boyd, *Experimental Mathematics* 8 (1999), 381–394. |
| December | **Mock theta functions.** | **Srinivasa Ramanujan.** His final letter to Hardy introduced functions that looked modular but did not fit the existing theory; Zwegers later explained them through harmonic Maass forms. | [Mock theta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mock_theta_function) · S. Zwegers, *Mock Theta Functions* (PhD thesis, 2002). |

## Video directions

January should directly compare equally spaced versus Chebyshev interpolation; March can grow a dessin as the preimage of `[0,1]`; July/August make a Meijer-G pair; November can start from a flat plate and end by zooming into the Blasius solution's complex singularities.
