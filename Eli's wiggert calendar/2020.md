# Complex Beauties 2020 — lesson seeds

Primary source: Wegert, Semmler, Gorkin & Daepp, *Complex Beauties 2020*, author-uploaded full text; [official archive](https://blogs.hrz.tu-freiberg.de/mathekalender/english/). Calendar assets remain external.

| Month | Function / pictured object | Person / historical story | Reading and citation trail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| January | **Hermite polynomials `He_n(z)`.** | **Charles Hermite.** Orthogonal polynomials born from analysis now appear throughout Gaussian probability and the quantum harmonic oscillator; complex zeros and growth give them a different visual life. | [Hermite polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermite_polynomials) · NIST DLMF Chapter 18. |
| February | **Finite Blaschke product and Brouwer fixed point theorem.** | **L. E. J. Brouwer.** A topological existence theorem guarantees a fixed point, while the Blaschke product lets us see a rigid analytic special case and then ask what topology alone knew. | [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brouwer_fixed-point_theorem) · [Blaschke product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaschke_product). |
| March | **Riesz function** `R(x)=Σ_{k≥1}(-1)^{k+1}x^k/((k-1)!ζ(2k))`. | **Marcel Riesz.** Riesz gave a criterion equivalent to the Riemann hypothesis expressed as a growth bound on this entire function. | [Riesz function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riesz_function) · Riesz's 1916 paper on the RH criterion. |
| April | **Complex Taylor-series version of the Gibbs phenomenon**, based on `log((1+z)/(1-z))`. | **J. Willard Gibbs**—but with **Henry Wilbraham's 1848 priority** for the overshoot phenomenon and Maxime Bôcher's later naming history. | [Gibbs phenomenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibbs_phenomenon) · Hewitt & Hewitt, *Archive for History of Exact Sciences* 21(2) (1979). |
| May | **Denjoy–Wolff iteration of a degree-2 Blaschke product**, with the eighth iterate approaching the boundary fixed point `e^(πi/3)`. | **Arnaud Denjoy.** Denjoy and Wolff independently identified the unique attracting point governing iterates of a disk self-map with no interior fixed point. | [Denjoy–Wolff theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denjoy%E2%80%93Wolff_theorem) · Cowen & MacCluer, *Composition Operators on Spaces of Analytic Functions* (1995). |
| June | **Poisson distribution CDF complexified:** `F_z(10)=e^{-z}Σ_{k=0}^{10}z^k/k!`. | **Siméon Denis Poisson.** A discrete probability law becomes an entire function once its mean is allowed to be complex; the zeros then become a legitimate analytic object. | [Poisson distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_distribution) · [Incomplete gamma function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incomplete_gamma_function). |
| July | **Fabry gap series**, e.g. `Σ z^{k^2}`, with the unit circle as a natural boundary. | **Eugène Fabry.** Sparse exponents can prevent analytic continuation across every point of the convergence circle; Pólya later supplied a converse direction under related hypotheses. | [Fabry gap theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabry_gap_theorem) · literature on Hadamard/Fabry gap theorems and Pólya's converse. |
| August | **Green's function / Laplace boundary-value problem**, using a series solution from Lloyd Trefethen's computational work. | **George Green.** A self-taught miller turned potential theory into integral identities and Green's functions; the calendar's modern computation makes that nineteenth-century idea executable. | [Green's function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green%27s_function) · Trefethen, “Series Solution of Laplace Problems,” *ANZIAM J.* 60 (2018), 1–26. |
| September | **Kolmogorov–Smirnov distribution/test**, extended to a complex variable. | **Andrey Kolmogorov.** The supremum distance between empirical and reference CDFs has a universal limiting distribution; complexifying its formulas reveals the analytic machinery beneath a standard statistics test. | [Kolmogorov–Smirnov test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolmogorov%E2%80%93Smirnov_test) · Smirnov's classical distribution theory. |
| October | **Preholomorphic / discrete analytic functions.** | **Jacqueline Ferrand.** Discrete analogues of Cauchy–Riemann behavior let complex analysis survive on grids and combinatorial structures. | [Discrete complex analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_complex_analysis) · Pierre Pansu, profile of Ferrand, *Notices AMS* 65(2) (2018). |
| November | **Phragmén–Lindelöf principle.** | **Lars Edvard Phragmén.** The maximum-modulus principle can be pushed to unbounded regions if growth is controlled; the boundary can still dominate the interior. | [Phragmén–Lindelöf principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phragm%C3%A9n%E2%80%93Lindel%C3%B6f_principle) · standard entire-function texts. |
| December | **Liouville arithmetic function** `λ(n)=(-1)^Ω(n)` and Dirichlet series `ζ(2s)/ζ(s)`. | **Joseph Liouville.** This is the number-theoretic Liouville function, not his complex-analysis theorem; its cancellation is closely tied to prime distribution and the Riemann hypothesis. | [Liouville function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liouville_function) · [Dirichlet series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_series). |

## Video directions

April should explicitly credit Wilbraham before Gibbs; May can iterate the Blaschke product while marking the Denjoy–Wolff point; June is a good “what happens if a probability parameter becomes complex?” Short; August can start with Green's mill and end with a computed harmonic solution.
