# Complex Beauties 2015 — source-audit lesson seeds

Primary source: Wegert, Semmler, Gorkin & Daepp, *Complex Beauties 2015*, DOI [10.13140/2.1.3025.7442](https://doi.org/10.13140/2.1.3025.7442), plus the [official archive](https://blogs.hrz.tu-freiberg.de/mathekalender/english/). | April | **Fritz Noether topic — exact picture audit.** | **Fritz Noether.** Emmy Noether's brother worked in applied mathematics and fluid mechanics before being executed in the Soviet Union during the Great Purge; the calendar's exact function should anchor that history rather than the family connection alone. | [Fritz Noether](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_Noether). |
| May | **Wiener topic — exact picture audit.** Candidate graph includes Fourier analysis, Wiener algebra/Tauberian theorem and Wiener–Hopf methods. | **Norbert Wiener.** His work tied harmonic analysis to stochastic processes, communication and systems; verify which analytic function the calendar actually uses. | [Norbert Wiener](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norbert_Wiener) · [Wiener's tauberian theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiener%27s_tauberian_theorem). |
| June | **Eisenstein series.** | **Ferdinand Gotthold Max Eisenstein.** Modular forms turn a lattice sum into a holomorphic function with rigid transformation laws; Eisenstein's short life produced major work in both number theory and elliptic functions. | [Eisenstein series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisenstein_series) · [Eisenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gotthold_Eisenstein). |

| August | **Rational approximation on difficult compact sets / Swiss-cheese phenomena.** **Exact picture audit** for the particular set/function. | **Alice Roth.** Roth made important contributions to approximation by rational functions; her career and later rediscovery are also a useful history of work that nearly disappeared from the standard story. | [Alice Roth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alice_Roth) · literature on rational approximation and Swiss-cheese sets. |
| September | **Splines.** **Exact picture audit** for the spline/function shown. | **Isaac Jacob Schoenberg.** Schoenberg coined “spline” for piecewise-polynomial approximation and developed cardinal splines; the construction is ideal for animating local polynomial pieces joining smoothly. | [Spline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spline_(mathematics)) · [Isaac Jacob Schoenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Jacob_Schoenberg). |
| October | **Dedekind eta function `η(τ)`.** | **Richard Dedekind.** An infinite product with modular transformation laws connects partitions, modular forms and complex lattices. | [Dedekind eta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dedekind_eta_function) · [Richard Dedekind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dedekind). |
