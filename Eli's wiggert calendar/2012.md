# Complex Beauties 2012 — source-audit lesson seeds

Primary source: [TU Freiberg's official *Complex Beauties* archive](https://blogs.hrz.tu-freiberg.de/mathekalender/english/). An author-uploaded 2012 calendar is also listed as full text on Elias Wegert's ResearchGate profile. The current searchable copy does **not** expose every month reliably, so unresolved rows below are deliberately left unresolved instead of reconstructed from names or later calendars.

## Calendar rows recovered from the source trail

| Month | Function / pictured object | Person / historical story | Reading and citation trail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| March | **Finite Blaschke products.** Zeros in the unit disk generate rational inner functions whose boundary values have modulus one. | **Wilhelm Blaschke.** Blaschke products are the disk analogue of building an analytic function directly from its zeros. | [Blaschke product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaschke_product) · [Wilhelm Blaschke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Blaschke). |
| July | **Julia sets / polynomial iteration.** The 2013 calendar explicitly points back to the July 2012 complex-dynamics entry. | **Gaston Julia.** Julia and Fatou developed the early iteration theory that later fed directly into Mandelbrot-set dynamics. | [Julia set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_set) · [Gaston Julia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaston_Julia) · Milnor, *Dynamics in One Complex Variable*. |
| August | **Gamma function `Γ(z)`.** The calendar series later points back to the August 2012 gamma-function picture. | Historical line: **Euler → Legendre → Weierstrass**. Euler's integral and product viewpoints become a meromorphic continuation with poles at the non-positive integers. | [Gamma function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamma_function) · [Euler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonhard_Euler) · Whittaker & Watson, *A Course of Modern Analysis*. |
| October | **Bessel function `J_n` from the wave equation.** The 2013 Neumann-function entry explicitly says October 2012 treated the wave equation in polar coordinates and used `J_n`. | **Friedrich Bessel.** Separation of variables turns circular membrane modes into Bessel's differential equation. | [Bessel function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessel_function) · [Bessel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Bessel) · NIST DLMF, Chapter 10. |
| November | **Nonlinear first-order differential equation `y' = y^2 + x^2`**, treated through a substitution in the Bernoulli/Riccati tradition. | **Jakob Bernoulli.** The useful story is the seventeenth-century search for substitutions that turn nonlinear differential equations into tractable forms. | [Riccati equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riccati_equation) · [Bernoulli differential equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_differential_equation) · [Jacob Bernoulli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Bernoulli). |

## Video directions from the verified rows

A strong sequence is: put zeros inside the disk and build a March Blaschke product; jump to July and iterate a polynomial until its Julia boundary appears; derive October's `J_n` from a vibrating disk; then compare `J_n` with the 2013 Neumann `Y_n` entry as two independent solutions of the same ODE.
