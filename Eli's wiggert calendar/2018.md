# Complex Beauties 2018 — lesson seeds

Primary source: [official *Complex Beauties* archive](https://blogs.hrz.tu-freiberg.de/mathekalender/english/) and the author-uploaded 2018 calendar. Calendar assets remain external.

| Month | Function / pictured object | Person / historical story | Reading and citation trail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| January | **Fibonacci generating function** `z/(1-z-z^2)`. | **Fibonacci.** A recurrence becomes a rational function; its two poles encode the same characteristic roots that produce Binet's formula. | [Fibonacci sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibonacci_sequence) · [Generating function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generating_function). |
| February | **Sophie Germain identity** `a^4+4b^4=(a^2-2ab+2b^2)(a^2+2ab+2b^2)`. | **Sophie Germain.** Her work on Fermat's Last Theorem and elasticity grew alongside Chladni's vibrating-plate experiments; the factorization itself is short enough to animate line by line. | [Sophie Germain identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_Germain%27s_identity) · [Sophie Germain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_Germain). |
| March | **Holomorphic logarithm of the gamma function** on a slit domain. | **Jacques Philippe Marie Binet.** Binet's work on `log Γ` and asymptotics sits behind formulas now used for stable gamma-function evaluation. | [Gamma function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamma_function) · [Binet's log gamma formulas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamma_function#Binet's_gamma_function_formulas). |
| April | **Littlewood polynomials**, coefficients restricted to `±1`; the calendar uses a random degree-50 example and its zero cloud. | **John Edensor Littlewood.** Severe coefficient restrictions still produce rich global zero geometry and connect analysis, probability and extremal polynomial problems. | [Littlewood polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Littlewood_polynomial) · Borwein & Erdélyi, *Illinois J. Math.* 41 (1997); Reyna & Damelin, arXiv:1504.08058. |
| May | **Finite-difference differentiation error** for `cos z`, contrasting an ordinary real difference with a complex-step-style formula at tiny `h`. | **Richard Courant.** Courant's 1925 work helped establish finite-difference methods for PDEs; the complex plane exposes cancellation error as well as truncation error. | [Finite difference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_difference) · [Complex-step derivative approximation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_differentiation#Complex-variable_methods). |
| June | **Toeplitz and circulant matrices.** | **Otto Toeplitz.** Constant-diagonal structure converts linear algebra into Fourier analysis; eigenvalue patterns can be visualized through the associated symbol. | [Toeplitz matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toeplitz_matrix) · Toeplitz's 1911 *Mathematische Annalen* paper. |
| July | **Eichler generating function** `q∏(1-q^n)^2(1-q^{11n})^2`, linking modular forms and elliptic-curve point counts. | **Martin Eichler.** Modular forms became a bridge between analytic functions and arithmetic geometry long before the full modularity theorem. | [Martin Eichler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Eichler) · [Modular form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_form) · Eichler–Shimura relation. |
| August | **Critical points of polynomials / Walsh localization theorem.** | **Joseph L. Walsh.** The zeros of a polynomial constrain where the zeros of its derivative can go; compare with Gauss–Lucas and then tighten the geometry. | [Gauss–Lucas theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauss%E2%80%93Lucas_theorem) · [Joseph L. Walsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_L._Walsh). |
| September | **Sierpiński carpets/gaskets appearing as Julia sets of rational maps.** | **Wacław Sierpiński.** A purely topological fractal pattern reappears as the dynamically generated boundary of a rational function. | [Sierpiński carpet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierpi%C5%84ski_carpet) · Devaney, *Notices AMS* (Jan. 2004) on rational maps and Sierpiński curves. |
| October | **Bergman kernel**, pictured as a linear combination of seven kernels. Guest contribution by Albrecht Böttcher. | **Stefan Bergman.** A domain determines a reproducing kernel for square-integrable holomorphic functions; the kernel then becomes a computational tool for conformal mapping. | [Bergman kernel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergman_kernel) · standard texts on Bergman spaces. |
| November | **Dirichlet eta function** `η(s)=(1-2^{1-s})ζ(s)`. | **Peter Gustav Lejeune Dirichlet.** Alternating the zeta series gives a representation converging in a larger half-plane and a clean analytic-continuation lesson. | [Dirichlet eta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_eta_function) · [Dirichlet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Gustav_Lejeune_Dirichlet). |
| December | **Cantor bouquet** in exponential dynamics. | **Georg Cantor.** Cantor's set-theoretic name is attached historically, while the actual dynamics story comes from Julia sets of exponential maps: uncountably many disjoint “hairs” stretching to infinity. | [Cantor bouquet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantor_bouquet) · literature on exponential Julia sets and straight brushes. |

## Video directions

January can derive the rational generating function directly from the recurrence; April can randomize `±1` coefficients and watch zeros stay in a narrow annular region; May is an excellent numerical-error Short; September/December make a pair on classical fractals reappearing inside complex dynamics.
