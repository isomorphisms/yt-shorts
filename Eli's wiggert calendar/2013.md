# Complex Beauties 2013 — lesson seeds

Primary source: Elias Wegert and Gunter Semmler, *Complex Beauties 2013*, author-uploaded full text; [official archive](https://blogs.hrz.tu-freiberg.de/mathekalender/english/). Calendar assets remain external.

| Month | Function / pictured object | Person / historical story | Reading and citation trail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| January | **`p(z)=z^5-z+1` and solvability by radicals.** | **Niels Henrik Abel.** Cubics and quartics had formulas; Abel proved there is no general radicals formula for the quintic, opening the road to Galois theory. | [Abel–Ruffini theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abel%E2%80%93Ruffini_theorem) · [Abel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niels_Henrik_Abel). |
| February | **Mandelbrot iteration** `f_0(z)=z`, `f_n(z)=f_{n-1}(z)^2+z`; the calendar shows an early iterate rather than only the final set. | **Benoît Mandelbrot.** Parameter-space dynamics turns the question “does this orbit escape?” into a geometric boundary. | [Mandelbrot set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandelbrot_set) · Douady & Hubbard's work on polynomial dynamics. |
| March | **Continued fractions.** | **Alfred Pringsheim.** Continued fractions sit between approximation, analytic continuation, and convergence theory; Pringsheim developed major convergence results. | [Continued fraction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continued_fraction) · [Pringsheim's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pringsheim%27s_theorem). |
| April | **`sinc z = sin(z)/z` and sampling.** | **Claude Shannon.** Band-limited signals can be recovered from regularly spaced samples; the sinc function is the interpolation kernel. | [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyquist%E2%80%93Shannon_sampling_theorem) · Shannon, “Communication in the Presence of Noise,” *Proc. IRE* 37 (1949). |
| May | **Finite exponential/Fourier sum** `c_1e^{d_1z}+c_2e^{d_2z}+c_3e^{d_3z}`. | **Joseph Fourier.** Periodic real data becomes a sum of complex exponentials; moving off the real axis exposes the analytic functions behind Fourier series. | [Fourier series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourier_series) · Fourier, *Théorie analytique de la chaleur* (1822). |
| June | **Jacobi theta functions.** | **Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi.** Theta functions package lattice sums, modular transformations, and elliptic-function identities into one family. | [Theta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theta_function) · [Jacobi theta functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theta_function#Jacobi_theta_function). |
| July | **Rogers–Ramanujan continued fraction**, shown through a high-order finite approximation. | **Srinivasa Ramanujan.** A continued fraction unexpectedly meets q-series, partition identities, and modular functions. | [Rogers–Ramanujan continued fraction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogers%E2%80%93Ramanujan_continued_fraction) · Andrews & Berndt on Ramanujan's lost notebook. |
| August | **Neumann/Bessel `Y_n` functions**, the second independent solution of Bessel's equation, with a logarithmic branch. | **Carl Gottfried Neumann.** This deliberately follows October 2012's `J_n`: one ODE, two very different complex solutions. | [Bessel function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessel_function) · NIST DLMF §10.2–10.8. |
| September | **Airy equation**, with the calendar picturing `Bi'(z)/Bi(z)`. | **George Biddell Airy.** The Airy function arose from optics and became the universal local model near a simple turning point. | [Airy function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airy_function) · Airy, “On the Intensity of Light in the Neighbourhood of a Caustic” (1838). |
| October | **Fresnel integral** `F(z)=∫_0^z sin(t^2)dt`. | **Augustin-Jean Fresnel.** Diffraction turns into an oscillatory integral whose real trace is the Cornu spiral and whose complex extension gives a natural phase portrait. | [Fresnel integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fresnel_integral) · [Fresnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustin-Jean_Fresnel). |
| November | **Cauchy/winding-number integral approximated by quadrature**, hence by a rational function with poles at quadrature nodes. | **Roger Cotes.** Newton–Cotes formulas connect eighteenth-century numerical integration to modern rational approximations of contour integrals. | [Newton–Cotes formulas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton%E2%80%93Cotes_formulas) · Cotes, *Harmonia Mensurarum* (1722). |
| December | **Superposition of 21 radial complex waves** with alternating phases and `1/r` decay. | **Christiaan Huygens.** Huygens's wavefront construction becomes a literal complex superposition experiment. | [Huygens–Fresnel principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huygens%E2%80%93Fresnel_principle) · Huygens, *Traité de la lumière* (1690). |

## Video directions

January can morph a solvable quartic into the generic quintic question; April can reconstruct a band-limited curve from samples one sinc pulse at a time; August should be paired directly with the 2012 Bessel entry; September/October make a good Airy-versus-Fresnel optics pair.
