# Preferred visual sequence

The field itself must demonstrate the cusp before the explanatory curve is drawn.

- Early: close view while the truncated integral is still changing.
- By roughly the first third of the Short: reach the full field view.
- Middle: continue the mathematical build at the full view.
- Then: hold the finished, unannotated field long enough to inspect the naturally visible cusp.
- Only after that hold: show a cue such as `caustic overlay`, then add a dashed curve for `27x² + 8y³ = 0`.
- Final: hold the annotated comparison.

The overlay is evidence/explanation, not part of the computed domain coloring.
