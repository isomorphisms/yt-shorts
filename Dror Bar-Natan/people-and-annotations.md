# People and annotations

Primary source: **Dror Bar-Natan**, [People](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/People/) and [Annotations](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Annotations/) in the Academic Pensieve.

The People index is best read as a set of research-contact/collaboration folders, **not** as a list of coauthors. Likewise, Annotations are Bar-Natan's reading/working notes around other authors and papers, not claims of collaboration.

The Annotations index includes folders for Alekseev–Kawazumi–Kuno–Naef, Alekseev–Torossian, Bardakov, Bar-Natan, Etingof–Kazhdan, Furusho, Kauffman, Ozsváth, Pulmann–Ševera, Vergne, and others. These are useful citation trails when a project points into the same literature.

For future source work, follow the exact project page first, then use People/Annotations to recover context. Do not infer authorship or endorsement merely from a folder name.
