# Dror Bar-Natan — Academic Pensieve source notes

Primary source and primary credit: **Dror Bar-Natan**, [Academic Pensieve](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/).

This folder is a provenance-first catalog for future YouTube Shorts work. Its purpose is to understand and credit the mathematics before any script, animation, or visual treatment is proposed, and to make it easy to send viewers back to Bar-Natan's original pages.

Snapshot: 2026-08-24. The Pensieve describes itself as containing most of Bar-Natan's raw research notes, programs, images, and related material since 2008, plus older material. It is an open research notebook, not a polished publication index, so unfinished computations and false starts are part of the source.

## Rules for using these notes

- Link prominently to the exact Pensieve page used.
- Credit Dror Bar-Natan as the Pensieve author/curator.
- For joint projects, credit the collaborators too.
- Distinguish collaboration from citation: "Bar-Natan works with X" is different from "Bar-Natan cites or builds on X".
- Preserve upstream references where they can be identified reliably.
- Treat experimental notebook claims as notebook claims unless they are backed by a paper.
- A Pensieve-hosted or linked image is **not automatically licensed for reuse**. Check the original image/video/source before republishing media.
- **No video concepts live in this folder yet.** These are source and citation notes only.
