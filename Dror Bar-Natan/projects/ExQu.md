# ExQu — Expansions and Quadraticity for Groups

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/ExQu](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/ExQu/)

## What this is

ExQu asks how far the idea of a Taylor expansion can be transferred from functions to groups and, more broadly, to algebraic structures. The construction page frames questions of existence, uniqueness, separation, and quadraticity of such expansions.

The notebooks connect this viewpoint with finite-type invariants, lower central series, pure braid groups, group-ring augmentation filtrations, semidirect products, and Quillen/Mal'cev-style completions.

## What is being worked out

The conceptual move is to treat “expansion” as a functorial algebraic approximation rather than a calculus-specific trick. Quadraticity asks whether the resulting graded/algebraic structure is controlled by relations of degree two, which is closely tied to whether the expansion is tractable.
