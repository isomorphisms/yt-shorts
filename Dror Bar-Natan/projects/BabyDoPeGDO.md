## What this is

BabyDoPeGDO is a deliberately simplified, one-step version of the larger DoPeGDO machinery. The project page describes it as a fresh implementation and connects it to older notebooks, including work in the Roland van der Veen People folder.

The page is candid about intermediate failures: some versions fail already at low expansion order. That makes the folder valuable as a development record rather than only as a repository of finished results.

## What is being worked out

The notes aim to solve for ρ₁, ρ₂, and ρ₃ in a Heisenberg formalism while stripping away enough machinery that the algebra can be inspected directly. Conceptually, it is a minimal laboratory for understanding which parts of DoPeGDO are essential before scaling back up to the full engine.
