# APAI — A Perturbed-Alexander Invariant

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/APAI](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/APAI/)
- **Joint work:** Roland van der Veen
- **Dedication:** V. F. R. Jones (1952–2020), described there as a friend and mentor

## What this is

APAI studies an invariant usually denoted ρ₁ that stays strikingly close to the ordinary Alexander-polynomial calculation. Starting from the standard Alexander matrix A, the construction replaces a plain determinant-only calculation with a quadratic expression involving entries of A⁻¹. The Pensieve stresses that it is simple and fast while its full topological explanation remains incomplete.

That last point is important: this is exactly the sort of open-notebook project where computational evidence and conceptual understanding are developing together.

## What Bar-Natan and van der Veen are trying to understand

The notes compare ρ-type data with Khovanov and HOMFLYPT information and test how much discriminating power can be extracted from a very modest perturbation of Alexander machinery. The project is as much about explaining *why* the extra inverse-matrix term is topological as it is about computing it.
