- **Pensieve sources / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/SeifertBox](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/SeifertBox/) and [Projects/SemiSeifert](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/SemiSeifert/)
- **Related project:** [Signatures](Signatures.md)

## SeifertBox

A 2025 computational toolbox for representing and manipulating Seifert surfaces. The data model records a thick graph, band crossings, twists, and conversions to/from planar-diagram data; notebooks draw and reduce those surfaces and compute Δ.

## Semi-Seifert

This companion line studies a “semi-Seifert” formula for the Alexander polynomial and a degenerate-Gaussian tangle formalism, including hand checks of Reidemeister moves. It connects onward to HigherRank and talk material.

## What is being worked out

Together the folders turn classical Seifert-surface arguments into explicit data structures and executable transformations, then ask how much of the same formalism survives when the classical hypotheses are weakened.
