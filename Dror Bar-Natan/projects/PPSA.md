# PPSA — polynomial-time knot polynomials via solvable approximations

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/PPSA](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/PPSA/)
- **Joint work:** Roland van der Veen
- **Related paper:** Bar-Natan and van der Veen, [A polynomial time knot polynomial](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.04853)

## What this is

PPSA develops knot invariants by replacing a difficult Lie algebra with a tower of solvable approximations. The Pensieve emphasizes polynomial-time computability and links the method to the long line of Alexander-type invariants.

## Citation trail

The project explicitly follows ideas of **Lev Rozansky** and **Andrew Overbay**. It also links:

- Louis Kauffman and David Radford, [Oriented quantum algebras and invariants of knots and links](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.1214)
- Kazuo Habiro, [Bottom tangles and universal invariants](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0505219)

## What Bar-Natan and van der Veen are trying to demonstrate

The computational trade is the central point: sacrifice exact access to the full simple Lie algebra, retain successive solvable layers, and obtain knot polynomials that remain rich but admit much cheaper commutator/reordering calculations. Later DoPeGDO and higher-rank work grows naturally from this program.
