## What this is

FullDoPeGDO is the larger implementation that follows the stripped-down BabyDoPeGDO experiments. The folder includes an engine, tests and profiling, algebraic notebooks, knot computations, and a subfolder explicitly carrying “Roland's conventions.”

Visible topics include the center of a ybax algebra, classical R-matrix plus scattering terms, exponentiation/logarithms, knot computations, and source-term experiments.

## What is being worked out

This is the point where the conceptual DoPeGDO calculus becomes software infrastructure. The notebooks move repeatedly between algebraic identities, implementation choices, correctness checks, and actual knot calculations. It should therefore be cited as an evolving computational project, not as a single theorem.
