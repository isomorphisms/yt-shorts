# PoleDancing

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/PoleDancing](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/PoleDancing/)
- **Cited background:** Gwenael Massuyeau, [Formal descriptions of Turaev's loop operations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.03974)

## What this is

The folder contains “Pole Dancing” notes/PDFs and material described through surface diagrams. It also stores or points toward Massuyeau's work on formal descriptions of Turaev's intersection and self-intersection operations on loops; Drinfeld associators enter that theory through pure braid groups.

## What is being worked out

From the project index alone, the safest description is that Bar-Natan is developing/using a surface-diagram formalism in the neighborhood of Turaev loop operations and associator-based descriptions. A more specific theorem-level account should wait for a document-by-document read of the PDFs rather than being inferred from filenames.
