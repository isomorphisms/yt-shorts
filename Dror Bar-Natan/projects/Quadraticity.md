# Quadraticity / Quadraticity2

- **Pensieve sources / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/Quadraticity](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/Quadraticity/) and [Projects/Quadraticity2](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/Quadraticity2/)
- **Related paper:** Peter Lee, [The Pure Virtual Braid Group Is Quadratic](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.2356)

## What this is

These 2011-era notes study when topologically defined relations and syzygies produce quadratic algebraic presentations, especially around pure virtual braid groups and finite-type/expansion questions.

The folders refer to “Ideal-Theoretic Hutchings Theory,” topological and algebraic syzygies, a “Peter's Hexagon Lemma,” and explicit checks such as the B₄ syzygy.

## What is being worked out

The recurring experiment is to start with a geometric/topological relation, translate it into an algebraic relation among generators, and ask whether all higher consistency conditions are forced by low-degree (quadratic) data. Peter Lee's work is a direct citation trail and should be credited when that pure-virtual-braid result is discussed.
