# Arrow Diagrams and gl(N)

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/Arrow_Diagrams_and_gl(N)](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/Arrow_Diagrams_and_gl(N)/)
- **Joint project:** Louis Leung

## What this is

An older 2008–2009 symbolic-computation project on arrow diagrams and gl(N), with related Lie-bialgebra material. Mathematica notebooks build bases and relations, compute coproducts, and carry explicit calculations through low degrees.

## What is being worked out

The project turns diagrammatic relations into concrete linear-algebra/combinatorial computations. Rather than treating arrow-diagram spaces abstractly, the notebooks enumerate bases, impose relations, and inspect what survives. Credit Louis Leung whenever this material is used; the Pensieve identifies it as a joint project.
