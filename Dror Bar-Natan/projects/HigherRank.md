# HigherRank

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/HigherRank](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/HigherRank/)
- **Related work:** Roland van der Veen; APAI; Θ; Bonn/Geneva talks

## What this is

This 2024–2025 project pushes perturbed-Alexander/Θ-style machinery beyond rank one, especially toward A₂ / sl₃. The notebooks include a two-variable perturbed Alexander invariant, an A₂ degree-one invariant obtained by undetermined coefficients, formal or nilpotent Gaussian integration, and rank-two Burau/Gassner experiments.

A subfolder is explicitly named for Roland van der Veen, and some Θ computations refer to “Roland's choice of Feynman diagrams.”

## What is being worked out

The question is whether the efficient algebraic technology that works so well in the sl₂/Alexander setting survives in higher rank. Several notebooks test invariance directly by integration and explore which diagrams or integrands reproduce the desired low-order invariants.
