# Duflo

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/Duflo](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/Duflo/)
- **Paper:** Dror Bar-Natan, Zsuzsanna Dancso, and Nancy Scherich, [Ribbon 2-Knots, 1+1=2, and Duflo's Theorem for Arbitrary Lie Algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.08558)

## What this is

The project develops a topological/combinatorial route to the multiplicativity part of the Duflo isomorphism. The paper passes through ribbon 2-knots and arrow-diagram algebra: a seemingly elementary “1+1=2” operation on a four-dimensional abacus becomes an identity with Lie-algebraic content.

## Citation trail

The paper explicitly continues earlier Bar-Natan work with **Thang Le** and **Dylan Thurston** on a three-dimensional abacus/Kontsevich-integral picture, as well as earlier w-knotted-object work of Bar-Natan and **Zsuzsanna Dancso**.

## What is being worked out

The notebooks around the project examine formulas such as Zʷ and cap/uniqueness relations. The larger point is to make a difficult Lie-theoretic theorem emerge from operations on topological diagrams, rather than from the usual analytic/algebraic proof alone.
