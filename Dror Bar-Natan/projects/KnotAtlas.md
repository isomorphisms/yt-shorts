# KnotAtlas

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/KnotAtlas](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/KnotAtlas/)

## What this is

Historical computation and upload tooling connected to the Knot Atlas. Visible material includes HFK-hat data, Jones-polynomial computations for 13-crossing knots, multivariable Alexander calculations, and Khovanov table maintenance.

The folder also links work of **Cody Armond and Oliver Dasbach/Cohen-related collaborators** around knot polynomial calculations; because filenames/index links are not enough to settle every authorship detail, follow the linked paper before assigning specific credit.

## What it demonstrates

This is a data-pipeline project: compute invariants in bulk, check them, and publish them into a shared knot-information resource. It is a useful reminder that large knot tables are produced by software and curation, not just by hand calculations.
