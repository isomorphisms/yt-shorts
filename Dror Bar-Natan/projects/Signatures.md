## What this is

A collection of notebooks around signature-type knot invariants and matrix formulas. Topics include deriving the Alexander polynomial from an F×F matrix, knot signatures from a Goeritz matrix, Meyer cocycles, Tristram–Levine signatures, and comparisons with Kashaev-style constructions.

## What is being worked out

The project repeatedly asks whether familiar knot invariants can be rewritten in a common matrix/Gaussian language. Some notebooks explicitly search for comparisons or possible counterexamples, so individual experimental conclusions should be cited to the specific notebook rather than stated as settled facts.
