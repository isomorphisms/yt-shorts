# RibbonKnots

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/RibbonKnots](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/RibbonKnots/)
- **Related work:** APAI / ρ₁ and older ribbon-drawing projects

## What this is

A working folder for explicit ribbon-knot drawings and computations. It continues earlier “PolyPoly/Killam” material and includes calculations of the perturbed-Alexander invariant ρ₁ for ribbon knots through specified crossing ranges.

## What is being worked out

The project supplies concrete families on which the newer invariant machinery can be tested. The useful connection is between geometric ribbon presentations and algebraic data from ρ₁: one can ask whether the special geometry of ribbon knots leaves a recognizable signature in the invariant.
