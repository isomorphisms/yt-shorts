# PlanetHopf

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/PlanetHopf](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/PlanetHopf/)
- **Related contributor/reference:** Mohammad Javad Azadi, whose alternative views are linked from the Pensieve

## What this is

A visual/computational construction that pulls geographic imagery from Planet Earth back through the Hopf fibration. The folder contains Mathematica code, still frames, and movie files, including runs based on selected countries/continents.

## What it demonstrates

The Hopf fibration is difficult to picture because its natural domain is the 3-sphere. Texturing the construction with familiar Earth geography gives the fibers and their linking a concrete visual reference. The Pensieve is useful here as both source code and a record of experiments.

Do not assume the geographic source imagery or linked alternative videos are freely reusable merely because they appear on or are linked from the Pensieve.
