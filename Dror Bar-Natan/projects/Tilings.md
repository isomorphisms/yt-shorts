# Tilings

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/Tilings](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/Tilings/)

## What this is

A large visual/experimental tilings archive, active again in 2026. It contains symmetry-pattern subfolders, contact sheets, Mathematica-generated images, and exploratory pages such as “A Klein Bottle Sofa.” The index also links the GeCla material at atractor.pt and Bar-Natan's own symmetry-tilings gallery.

## What is being worked out

The visible files suggest computational exploration of symmetry classes, phase-like changes among patterns, random permutations, and territorial/partition effects. Because filenames alone do not identify a single theorem or formal classification, these should be described as experiments unless a specific notebook or paper is read.

This is one of the Pensieve's most visually rich mathematical areas, but each externally sourced image still needs a license/provenance check.
