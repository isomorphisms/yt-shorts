# Braidors

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/Braidors](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/Braidors/)

## What this is

A 2016 collection of symbolic notebooks on “braidor equations” and associator-type structures. Visible computations include checking that B(Φ) solves the braidor equations, solving a fresh braidor to degree 10, and working with associators in TAUT/SAUT-like settings.

## What is being worked out

The project treats coherence equations as explicit formal power-series problems: posit an associator/braidor object, expand it to bounded degree, and verify or solve the relations computationally. A more precise conceptual account should follow the definitions in the notebooks rather than infer them from these filenames.
