# EmergentAssociators

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/EmergentAssociators](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/EmergentAssociators/)
- **Paper:** Yusuke Kuno, with an appendix by Dror Bar-Natan, [Emergent version of Drinfeld's associator equations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02549)

## What this is

Kuno studies an “emergent” version of Drinfeld's associator equations and a topological formulation using emergent braids. Bar-Natan contributes an appendix and maintains related Pensieve material.

## Citation trail

The paper sits near the route from associators to the Kashiwara–Vergne problem developed by **Anton Alekseev and Charles Torossian** and by **Alekseev, Benjamin Enriquez, and Torossian**. It is also adjacent to emergent-tangle/Goldman–Turaev work involving Bar-Natan, **Zsuzsanna Dancso, Jason Hogan, Leilei Liu, and Nancy Scherich**.

## What is being demonstrated

The idea is to weaken or linearize the associator equations while retaining enough structure to recover corresponding linearized Kashiwara–Vergne equations. The braid/tangle language makes those algebraic equations visible as topological consistency conditions.
