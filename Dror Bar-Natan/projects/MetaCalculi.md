# MetaCalculi

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/MetaCalculi](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/MetaCalculi/)

## What this is

The project collects a common program for several “w-meta-calculi”: compact algebraic calculi designed to encode operations on tangles and related objects. The Pensieve includes α-calculus trace computations and Γ-calculus checks.

One notebook verifies the full set of Kashiwara–Vergne equations in Γ-calculus; another checks a unitarity identity. The page also links a Terry Tao article, “Sweeping a matrix rotates its graph,” as related intuition/context.

## What is being worked out

The goal is compression: replace a large diagrammatic computation by a small collection of algebraic update rules and then verify deep consistency equations inside that calculus. The value of the notebooks is that those checks are executable rather than merely formal prose.
