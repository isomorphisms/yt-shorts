# YarnBallKnots

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Projects/YarnBallKnots](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Projects/YarnBallKnots/)
- **Paper:** Dror Bar-Natan, Itai Bar-Natan, Iva Halacheva, and Nancy Scherich, [Yarn Ball Knots and Faster Computations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10923)

## What this is

The project asks what happens computationally when a knot is represented using its genuinely three-dimensional structure rather than forced into a planar diagram from the start. The paper shows complexity savings for tasks including linking number and finite-type invariant computations.

The Pensieve folder includes many physical-knot photographs and a `TrefoilByTuring` image alongside the mathematical material.

## What is being demonstrated

Representation is an algorithmic choice. A 3D “yarn ball” representation can expose locality or structure that is hidden by a large planar projection, making some computations substantially cheaper.
