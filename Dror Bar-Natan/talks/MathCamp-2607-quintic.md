# MathCamp-2607 — quintics, roots, and Abel's theorem

- **Pensieve source / credit:** Dror Bar-Natan, [Talks/MathCamp-2607](https://drorbn.net/AcademicPensieve/Talks/MathCamp-2607/)

## What this is

A pedagogical sequence moving from quadratic, cubic, and quartic equations to the obstruction for solving the general quintic by radicals. The pages progress through roots and permutation/continuation behavior, then use commutators of permutations/paths to isolate the obstruction.

The quintic page says the historical problem was first settled by **Niels Henrik Abel** and **Évariste Galois**, and that the presentation follows the topological solution associated with **Vladimir Arnold**.

## What Bar-Natan is demonstrating

The talk recasts Abel–Ruffini as a topological/monodromy obstruction. Radical formulas can only build certain kinds of covering behavior; nested commutators in the quintic's root permutations survive beyond what such radical constructions can absorb. The path from explicit low-degree formulas to topology is part of the teaching design.
