## What this is

A working talk folder that continued to receive notes after the nominal 2025 talk date. Visible material develops rank-two / mod-ε² invariants, integration techniques, and candidate integrands for ρ₁ and Θ.

Some experiments explicitly record failure of Reidemeister-III-type behavior unless an additional “wisdom projection” is imposed. That is a sign of active mathematical development, not a flaw to hide.

## What is being worked out

The folder tracks attempts to extend Gaussian/integration techniques into higher-rank knot-invariant calculations while preserving invariance. Its main value for source study is showing the trial-and-error route by which candidate formulas are accepted, modified, or rejected.
