## What the source itself says it is doing

Greene describes the article as a story about Heegaard Floer homology: **how it developed, how it evolved, what it taught mathematicians, and where it may lead**.

That makes it potentially useful for Shorts in a different way from a one-object “What Is…?” piece: instead of extracting a whole survey, look for one concrete diagram, invariant, example, or historical transition at a time.
