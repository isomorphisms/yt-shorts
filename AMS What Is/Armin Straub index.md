# Armin Straub’s “What Is…?” index

## Source

- https://arminstraub.com/math/what-is-column
- AMS collection: https://www.ams.org/cgi-bin/notices/nxgnotices.pl?cnt=whatis&fm=gen

## Why this belongs here

Armin Straub assembled and maintains a chronological index to the *Notices of the AMS* **“What Is…?”** column. It is a retrieval/indexing source, not an AMS article by Straub.

The inspected version contains **174 entries**, from:

- Oleg Viro, **“What is…an amoeba?”**, September 2002

to:

- Luis A. Duffaut Espinosa, Kurusch Ebrahimi-Fard, W. Steven Gray, and Venkatesh G. S., **“What is…a Chen-Fliess series?”**, February 2026
- Simone Linz and Kristina Wicke, **“What is…phylogenetics?”**, February 2026

Straub’s page links the entries directly to AMS pages/PDFs and, for recent items, DOI/HTML versions. Use it as the main table of contents for future article-by-article passes.
