# Elias Wegert / *Complex Beauties* source archive

This file records the primary sources for using Elias Wegert's phase-portrait work in Shorts, especially the *Complex Beauties* mathematical calendars.

## Rights status

The official TU Bergakademie Freiberg calendar site offers the complete run for download, including all editions from 2011 through the final 2024 edition. That is evidence that the authors intend the calendars to be freely downloadable from their own archive.

I could not find an explicit Creative Commons, public-domain, or other redistribution license for the calendar PDFs or their images. The 2024 calendar carries an ordinary copyright notice naming Elias Wegert, Gunter Semmler, Pamela Gorkin, and Ulrich Daepp. Earlier calendars likewise carry copyright notices. Therefore **do not mirror the calendar PDFs or extract/rehost their images in this repository unless a separate permission or redistribution license is located**. Link to the authors' official copies instead.

Official archive: https://blogs.hrz.tu-freiberg.de/mathekalender/english/

2024 copyright/source note: https://blogs.hrz.tu-freiberg.de/mathekalender/wp-content/uploads/sites/117/2023/10/1_calendar2024Ger_no_margin.pdf

## Attribution by edition

For 2011–2013, cite **Elias Wegert and Gunter Semmler** as the calendar authors. The 2013 edition also thanks Pamela Gorkin and Ulrich Daepp for the English translation.

Beginning with the 2014 edition, the calendar team consists of **Elias Wegert, Gunter Semmler, Pamela Gorkin, and Ulrich Daepp**. Individual months sometimes have guest authors or separately credited numerical/visual contributions. If a Short uses a particular calendar page rather than merely citing the series, copy the credit for that month from the calendar itself as well as the four-person calendar-team credit.

Useful edition records:

- Wegert, Elias; Semmler, Gunter. *Complex Beauties 2011*. DOI: https://doi.org/10.13140/2.1.3650.7844
- Wegert, Elias; Semmler, Gunter. *Complex Beauties 2013*. DOI: https://doi.org/10.13140/2.1.4437.2160
- Wegert, Elias; Semmler, Gunter; Gorkin, Pamela; Daepp, Ulrich. *Complex Beauties 2014*. DOI: https://doi.org/10.13140/2.1.2864.3527
- Official 2024 calendar page and team credit: https://blogs.hrz.tu-freiberg.de/mathekalender/english/

## Background citations for Wegert's visualization method

When a Short is about the phase-portrait/domain-coloring method itself, cite the methodological work rather than citing only a calendar image:

- Elias Wegert and Gunter Semmler, “Phase Plots of Complex Functions: a Journey in Illustration,” *Notices of the American Mathematical Society* 58(6), 768–780 (2011). Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.2295
- Elias Wegert, *Visual Complex Functions: An Introduction with Phase Portraits*, Birkhäuser/Springer Basel (2012), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-0348-0180-5

## Short-description attribution template

For a Short whose image is newly rendered with our Wegert-derived renderer rather than copied from a calendar, distinguish the implementation from the underlying visual method. A compact credit is:

> Phase-portrait/domain-coloring method after Elias Wegert and Gunter Semmler; see arXiv:1007.2295. *Complex Beauties* calendar archive by Wegert, Semmler, Gorkin, and Daepp: official Freiberg archive linked in the source notes.

Do not describe a new render as a calendar image unless it actually reproduces a specific credited calendar work.
