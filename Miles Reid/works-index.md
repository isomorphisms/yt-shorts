# Miles Reid — working index of papers and books

Primary source: Miles Reid's own current list of works:
https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Personal/works

The numbered list below follows Reid's numbering **1–54** so that later source reads can be checked directly against his bibliography.

Status labels:

- **source-read** — substantial source/abstract material inspected in this pass.
- **contents-read** — contents/section structure inspected.
- **index-only** — bibliographic/title-level note only; do not treat the one-line description as a theorem summary.

## 1972–1987: surfaces, K3s and the birth of the 3-fold program

### 1. The complete intersection of two or more quadrics — PhD thesis (1972)

Source: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/3folds/qu.pdf

**index-only.** Early work on the geometry of complete intersections of quadratic equations. Later literature continues to cite it for the relation between intersections/pencils of quadrics, linear spaces on them and associated hyperelliptic/Jacobian geometry.

### 2. Hyperelliptic linear systems on a K3 surface (1976)

Source: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Reprint/hellK3.pdf

**index-only.** K3-surface linear systems, specifically the hyperelliptic case. Natural follow-up questions: how a divisor on a K3 maps the surface, when the map is 2-to-1, and how curve geometry controls the linear system.

### 3. Special linear systems on curves lying on a K3 surface (1976)

Source: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Reprint/sK3.pdf

**index-only.** Curves on K3 surfaces and special linear systems. Should be read alongside item 2 rather than independently guessed from the title.

### 4. Elliptic Gorenstein surface singularities (1975, unpublished)

Source listed by Reid: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/surf/eG.ps

**index-only.** Early singularity work later echoed in Chapter 4 of *Chapters on Algebraic Surfaces*: elliptic Gorenstein singularities as a class generalizing Du Val/rational singularities.

### 5. Bogomolov's theorem `c₁² ≤ 4c₂` (1977)

Source: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Reprint/Bogomolov.pdf

**index-only.** A surface inequality involving Chern numbers. Needs direct reading before extracting proof ideas or historical attribution.

### 6. Surfaces with `p_g = 0, K² = 1` (1978)

Source: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Reprint/pg0K21.pdf

**index-only.** Numerical Godeaux-type territory: surfaces of general type with very small invariants.

### 7. Surfaces with `p_g = 0, K² = 2` (1978, unpublished)

Source listed by Reid: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/surf/K2=2.ps

**index-only.** Small-invariant general-type surfaces, close to Campedelli territory.

### 8. `π₁` for surfaces with small `K²` (1979)

Source: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Reprint/pi_1.pdf

**index-only.** Fundamental groups constrained by small canonical self-intersection.

### 9. Canonical 3-folds (1980)

Listed at: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/3folds/

**index-only.** One of the early papers in Reid's shift from surface classification to three-dimensional birational geometry.

### 10. Lines on Fano 3-folds according to Shokurov (1980, unpublished)

Source listed by Reid: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Unpub/five_early/Shok.ps

**index-only.** Geometry of lines on Fano 3-folds following Shokurov. Reid flags errors to fix, so do not use it casually without checking.

### 11. Minimal models of canonical 3-folds (1983)

Listed at: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/3folds/

**index-only.** Minimal-model machinery for 3-folds: a core historical item in the higher-dimensional birational program.

### 12. Decomposition of toric morphisms (1983)

Bibliography: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Personal/works

**index-only.** Toric birational maps/morphisms decomposed into simpler pieces. Likely valuable as an explicit combinatorial model for more general birational transformations.

### 13. Projective morphisms according to Kawamata (1983, unpublished)

Source: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/3folds/Ka.pdf

**index-only.** Exposition/work around Kawamata's projective morphism techniques in the emerging minimal-model program.

### 14. Surfaces of small degree (1986)

Bibliography: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Personal/works

**index-only.** Low-degree projective surfaces and the strong constraints that low degree imposes on their geometry.

### 15. Tendencious survey of 3-folds (1987)

Bibliography: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Personal/works

**index-only.** Survey/perspective source. Useful for Reid's view of the field at the time, but historical framing should be cross-checked.

### 16. Young person's guide to canonical singularities (1987)

Listed at: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/3folds/

**index-only in this pass.** Major expository reference on canonical/terminal singularities in the 3-fold minimal-model program. This deserves a dedicated full read later.

### 17. Undergraduate Algebraic Geometry (1988)

Cambridge contents: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/undergraduate-algebraic-geometry/contents/916690500E360CCA5652923CE927F26E

**contents-read.** Detailed notes in [`books/Undergraduate Algebraic Geometry.md`](books/Undergraduate%20Algebraic%20Geometry.md). Strong material: conics, cubic group law, Pascal, Nullstellensatz, rational maps, tangent spaces, blowups and the 27 lines on a cubic surface.

### 18. The moduli space of 3-folds with `K = 0` may nevertheless be irreducible (1987)

Bibliography: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Personal/works

**index-only.** A moduli-space statement for Calabi–Yau-style `K=0` threefolds. Read directly before stating what hypotheses or irreducibility result are intended.

## 1988–1999: canonical surfaces, textbooks and McKay

### 19. Quadrics through a canonical surface (1990)

Source: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Reprint/quads.pdf

**index-only.** Quadratic equations containing canonically embedded surfaces; a bridge between intrinsic surface invariants and equations of an embedding.

### 20. Infinitesimal view of extending a hyperplane section (1990)

Source: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Reprint/hyperplane.pdf

**index-only.** Deformation/extension questions: when a variety can arise as a hyperplane section of something larger, studied infinitesimally.

### 21. Campedelli versus Godeaux (1991)

Bibliography: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Personal/works

**index-only.** Comparison/construction questions for two classical families of surfaces of general type with small invariants.

### 22. Surfaces with `p_g=3, K²=4` according to E. Horikawa and D. Dicks (1989/1990 proceedings)

Surface page: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/surf/

**index-only.** Explicit canonical-ring/surface construction cluster based on Horikawa and Duncan Dicks.

### 23. Nonnormal del Pezzo surfaces (1994)

Bibliography: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Personal/works

**index-only.** del Pezzo surfaces after dropping normality; a reminder that classification changes sharply when standard regularity hypotheses are removed.

### 24. Undergraduate Commutative Algebra (1995)

Cambridge contents/index: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/undergraduate-commutative-algebra/index/5EA6706954B2E43A3611E548DB97707E

**contents-read.** Detailed notes in [`books/Undergraduate Commutative Algebra.md`](books/Undergraduate%20Commutative%20Algebra.md): ideals, `Spec`, modules, Noetherian rings, Nullstellensatz, localisation, primary decomposition, DVRs and normality.

### 25. Ito–Reid, The McKay correspondence for finite subgroups of `SL(3,C)` (1996)

McKay page: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/McKay/

**index-only.** Extension of the classical 2D/ADE McKay story into dimension three. Credit **Yukari Ito and Miles Reid**.

### 26. Chapters on Algebraic Surfaces (1997)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/alg-geom/9602006

**contents-read.** Detailed notes in [`books/Chapters on Algebraic Surfaces.md`](books/Chapters%20on%20Algebraic%20Surfaces.md): cubic surfaces, scrolls, intersection theory, sheaves/cohomology, K3s, singularities, Mori theory and classification.

### 27. Catanese–Franciosi–Hulek–Reid, Embeddings of curves and surfaces (1999)

Bibliography: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Personal/works

**index-only.** Criteria/problems around embeddings of curves and surfaces. Credit **Fabrizio Catanese, Marco Franciosi, Klaus Hulek and Miles Reid**.

### 28. Graded rings over K3 surfaces (1996 conference abstract)

Surface page: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/surf/

**index-only.** K3 surfaces encoded by graded rings; a direct precursor to later Fano/K3 graded-ring construction work.

### 29. McKay correspondence (1997)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/alg-geom/9702016

**source-read at abstract level.** Reid emphasizes numerical examples, representations versus cohomology, conjugacy classes versus homology, mirror-symmetry motivation and Nakamura's `G`-Hilbert scheme.

### 30. Bridgeland–King–Reid, The McKay correspondence as an equivalence of derived categories (2001)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9908027

**abstract/index-level.** Derived McKay correspondence: a categorical strengthening of the numerical correspondence. Credit **Tom Bridgeland, Alastair King and Miles Reid**.

### 31. Craw–Reid, How to calculate `A-Hilb C³` (2002)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9909085

**source-read at abstract/result-summary level.** A particularly visual algorithm using continued fractions and regular tessellations by equilateral triangles. Credit **Alastair Craw and Miles Reid**. Detailed map: [`topics/McKay-correspondence.md`](topics/McKay-correspondence.md).

### 32. La correspondance de McKay — Bourbaki seminar (2002)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9911165

**index-level.** Expository McKay seminar; companion homework is linked at https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Homework/.

## 2000–2005: explicit birational geometry, graded rings and unprojection

### 33. Corti–Pukhlikov–Reid, Birationally rigid Fano hypersurfaces (2000)

Bibliography: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Personal/works

**index-only.** Birational rigidity of Fano hypersurfaces. Credit **Alessio Corti, Aleksandr Pukhlikov and Miles Reid**.

### 34. 25 years of 3-folds — and old person's view (2000)

Bibliography: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Personal/works

**index-only.** Historical/survey account of the development of 3-fold birational geometry.

### 35. Papadakis–Reid, Kustin–Miller unprojection without complexes (2004)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0011094

**source-read at abstract level.** Recasts Kustin–Miller unprojection in a form adapted to birational geometry, using dualising-sheaf/adjunction ideas. Credit **Stavros Papadakis and Miles Reid**, and retain attribution to **Andrew Kustin and Matthew Miller** for the original theorem.

### 36. Graded rings and birational geometry (2000 proceedings)

PDF: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/3folds/Ki/Ki.pdf

**source-read at abstract level.** Canonical/anticanonical graded rings, Gorenstein structure, projection/unprojection, Fano 3-fold construction and serial unprojection.

### 37. Examples of Type IV unprojection (2001)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0108037

**index-only.** Explicit examples of another unprojection format; read after item 35/36.

### 38. Altınok–Brown–Reid, Fano 3-folds, K3 surfaces and graded rings (2002)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202092

**index/abstract-level.** Explicit graded-ring construction linking K3 surfaces and Fano 3-folds. Credit **Selma Altınok, Gavin Brown and Miles Reid**.

### 39. Corti–Reid, Weighted Grassmannians (2002)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0206011

**source-read at abstract level.** Weighted analogues of homogeneous spaces such as Grassmannians used as ambient spaces for explicit constructions. Credit **Alessio Corti and Miles Reid**.

### 40. Update on 3-folds (ICM 2002)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0206157

**index-only.** Survey/status report on 3-fold birational geometry around 2002.

### 41. Reid–Suzuki, Cascades of projections from log del Pezzo surfaces (2003)

Bibliography: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Personal/works

**index-only.** Projection sequences/cascades for log del Pezzo surfaces. Credit **Miles Reid and Kaori Suzuki**.

### 42. Constructing algebraic varieties via commutative algebra (2005)

PDF: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/surf/ECM4.pdf

**source-read at abstract level.** Build varieties from canonical/anticanonical graded rings; use Gorenstein commutative algebra and computer algebra to attack existence/classification questions.

### 43. Reid–Szendrői, Geometry and Topology (2005)

Authors' page: https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/szendroi/reid_szendroi_geometry_topology.html

**contents-read.** Detailed map in [`books/Geometry and Topology.md`](books/Geometry%20and%20Topology.md). Credit **Miles Reid and Balázs Szendrői**.

## 2009–present: explicit surfaces/Fanos, codimension 4 and databases

### 44. Mendes Lopes–Pardini–Reid, Campedelli surfaces with fundamental group of order 8 (2009)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.0006

**index-only.** Construction/classification result for Campedelli surfaces with a specified fundamental-group size. Credit **Margarida Mendes Lopes, Rita Pardini and Miles Reid**.

### 45. Brown–Kerber–Reid, Fano 3-folds in codimension 4, Tom and Jerry, Part I (2012)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.4313

**index/abstract-level.** Codimension-4 Gorenstein constructions using two unprojection formats nicknamed Tom and Jerry. Credit **Gavin Brown, Michael Kerber and Miles Reid**.

### 46. Prokhorov–Reid, On Q-Fano threefolds of Fano index 2 (2016)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.0852

**index-only.** Numerical/structural restrictions and classification questions for Q-Fano 3-folds of index 2. Credit **Yuri Prokhorov and Miles Reid**.

### 47. Gorenstein in codimension 4 — the general structure theory (2015)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.5248

Companion page: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/codim4/

**resource-page read.** Structure of codimension-4 Gorenstein rings, with computational examples and Magma material.

### 48. Buckley–Reid–Zhou, Ice cream and orbifold Riemann–Roch (2013)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.0457

**source-read at abstract level.** Decomposes Hilbert series into integral Gorenstein-symmetric pieces; local orbifold corrections are “ice cream functions”. Credit **Anita Buckley, Miles Reid and Shengtian Zhou**.

### 49. Brown–Reid, Diptych varieties I (2013)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.2446

**index-only.** Explicit higher-codimension/birational construction cluster connected to unprojection and Mori flips. Credit **Gavin Brown and Miles Reid**.

### 50. Brown–Reid, Diptych varieties II: Apolar varieties (2017)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.5858

**index-only.** Continuation of the Diptych construction program. Credit **Gavin Brown and Miles Reid**.

### 51. Brown–Reid–Stevens, Tutorial on Tom and Jerry (2021)

arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.02594

**source-read at abstract level.** Introductory unprojection tutorial centered on the two smoothing components of the anticanonical cone over `P(1,2,3)`. Credit **Gavin Brown, Miles Reid and Jan Stevens**.

### 52. The Tate–Oort group scheme `TO_p` (2019)

Resource page: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/TOp/

Author manuscript: https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/121887/7/WRAP-Tate-Oort-group-scheme-Reid-2019.pdf

**source-read at abstract level.** Packages the order-`p` cyclic, multiplicative and additive group schemes into one family; develops representation theory and applications to torsion phenomena, Godeaux surfaces and Calabi–Yau examples in characteristic `p`.

### 53. Reid–Suzuki, Fano 3-folds of Fano index `≥ 3`, and Constructing GRDB Number 41058

Current bibliography entry: https://mreid.warwick.ac.uk/Personal/works

**index-only.** Current explicit Fano/GRDB construction paper. Credit **Miles Reid and Kaori Suzuki**; consult the published Taiwanese Journal version before extracting theorem details.

### 54. Brown–Kasprzyk–Reid, Kawamata bound for Fano threefolds and the Graded Ring Database

Current manuscript: https://kasprzyk.work/research/pdf/grdb.pdf

**source-read at abstract level.** Effective/computational form of Kawamata's bound producing **39,550 candidate Hilbert series** satisfying the stated stability inequality. Candidate series are not all claimed to be realised by Fano 3-folds. Credit **Gavin Brown, Alexander Kasprzyk and Miles Reid**.
