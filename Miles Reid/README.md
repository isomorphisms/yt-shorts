# Miles Reid

Source-research folder for material by **Miles Reid** (algebraic geometry, especially surfaces, 3-folds, singularities, graded rings and the McKay correspondence).

This is research substrate for the YouTube Shorts archive, not a set of finished scripts. The goal is to preserve credit, links, and small mathematical idea units that can later be turned into visual explanations.

## Source-status convention

These notes deliberately distinguish levels of confidence.

- **source-read**: abstract, chapter text, or substantial source material was inspected.
- **contents-read**: table of contents / section list was inspected, but not the full work.
- **index-only**: item is present in Reid's own list of works, but this pass did not inspect the paper itself.

Do not turn an **index-only** one-line description into a factual claim about a theorem. It is only a retrieval/indexing aid.

## Strong source clusters for later work

A first pass suggests several unusually dense sources for many independent mathematical explanations:

1. **Undergraduate Algebraic Geometry** — conics, cubic curves, the elliptic-curve group law, Pascal's theorem, Nullstellensatz, rational maps, blowups, and the 27 lines on a cubic surface.
2. **Chapters on Algebraic Surfaces** — cubic surfaces again at a more advanced level, rational scrolls, intersection theory, sheaves/cohomology, K3 surfaces, surface singularities and Mori theory.
3. **Surface cyclic quotient singularities** — invariant monomials, continued fractions, explicit resolutions, and toric geometry.
4. **Du Val singularities** — the ADE list, resolutions, quotient singularities, canonical class, numerical cycles.
5. **How to calculate A-Hilb C^3** (with Alastair Craw) — continued fractions plus regular tessellations by equilateral triangles.
6. **Ice cream and orbifold Riemann–Roch** (with Anita Buckley and Shengtian Zhou) — decomposing Hilbert series into integral Gorenstein-symmetric pieces called “ice cream functions”.
7. **Tom and Jerry** (with Gavin Brown, Michael Kerber / Jan Stevens in related papers) — concrete unprojection constructions in codimension 4.
8. **Geometry and Topology** (with Balázs Szendrői) — a large independent supply of visual topics: Euclidean motions, spherical/hyperbolic geometry, projective duality, quotient topology, winding number, quaternions, SU(2), Stern–Gerlach and transformation groups.

## Scope of this pass

Reid's own list currently has 54 numbered works, plus translations, edited books, and works in progress. This folder records all 54 at index level and goes substantially deeper on the most naturally decomposable books and topic clusters. A future pass can continue paper-by-paper from the index without changing the structure here.
