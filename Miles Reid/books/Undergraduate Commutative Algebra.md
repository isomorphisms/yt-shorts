Reid says the book grew from Warwick courses and deliberately contrasts abstract commutative algebra with concrete use in algebraic geometry and algebraic number theory.

## 0. Hello!

Concept atoms:

- `Z` versus `k[X]`: arithmetic and polynomial geometry as parallel examples of commutative rings.
- Why commutative algebra is the common language behind algebraic number theory and algebraic geometry.
- Concrete examples before abstraction.

- radical of an ideal;
- zero divisors;
