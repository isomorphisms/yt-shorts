# Geometry and Topology

**Miles Reid and Balázs Szendrői**, *Geometry and Topology*, Cambridge University Press (2005).

- Authors' book page: https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/szendroi/reid_szendroi_geometry_topology.html
- Cambridge contents: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/geometry-and-topology/contents/ABE70EEF7E28A5AA1DEAA7D4D529EAFC

Status: **contents-read**.

This is not narrowly algebraic geometry. It is a particularly strong source for visual mathematics because the book builds geometry around coordinate systems, transformations and symmetry, then moves through topology to quaternions and physics.
