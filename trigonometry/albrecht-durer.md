# Albrecht Dürer — measurement, geometry, perspective

Dürer belongs in this folder as a historical **measurement / perspective / geometry** source, not because *Underweysung der Messung* should be mislabeled as a modern trigonometry textbook.

## Primary object

Albrecht Dürer, *Underweysung der messung mit dem zirckel un richt scheyt* (*Instruction / Treatise on Measurement*), Nuremberg, 1525.

The Metropolitan Museum of Art describes the book as a synthesis of classical and contemporary mathematical material with Dürer's practical geometry, intended for painters but also goldsmiths, sculptors, stonemasons, carpenters, and others who use measurement. The Met marks its copy/images public domain.

## Strong Short seed: the perspective apparatus

The British Museum has Dürer's 1525 woodcut of an artist drawing a lute with a perspective apparatus. This is a particularly good bridge from Dürer's constructive geometry to elementary trigonometry.

Source: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/P_E-2-398

Possible reconstruction:

1. Show the real lute/object on one side of a vertical picture plane.
2. Fix the viewer's eye point.
3. Draw one sight ray from the eye to a selected point of the lute.
4. Mark where the ray pierces the picture plane.
5. Repeat for a handful of points; the projected outline appears.
6. Move the eye point sideways or closer/farther away and let the projected locations change.
7. Only then draw the right triangle that relates lateral displacement, depth, and viewing angle.

For a simple 2-D slice, if the picture plane is a perpendicular distance `d` from the eye and a ray makes angle `θ` from the central perpendicular, its displacement on the plane is `x = d tan θ`. This connects directly to the laser-pointer Short: both are ray-plane intersection problems governed by tangent.

## Another visual seed: grid / drawing frame

Dürer's perspective devices are compelling because they make a normally invisible geometric operation physical: lines of sight become strings/rays, and a projection becomes a mark on a frame or plane. Rebuild the device as clean geometry instead of relying on a static history slide.

A later pass should inspect the relevant book pages one by one and make proposition/device-sized notes rather than treating “Dürer” as a single biography Short.
