# Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons trigonometry image attribution

These files are a source-image pack for visual experiments and later YouTube Shorts. They are the non-formula media files used by the English Wikipedia article **Trigonometry** as checked on 2026-08-24:

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigonometry

The copies in `assets/` are downloaded from the current Wikimedia Commons file versions by `download.sh`. We do not claim authorship. For files with a visible revision history, substantive image edits are credited separately from administrative transfer to Commons.

When an image is changed for a Short, record that change in the Short's own credits as well. Share-alike files remain subject to their stated licenses.
